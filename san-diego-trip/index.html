Day by day

The itinerary

Four consecutive Go City pass days run from Thursday, December 17 through Sunday, December 20.

  1. Wed · Dec 16Arrival · No pass

    Waterfront welcome

    Land at 1:04 PM, travel to the hotel, leave luggage, and check in when the room is available. Keep the afternoon flexible for a flight delay.

    • Explore the Marriott marina and Embarcadero.
    • Walk through Seaport Village and Children’s Park.
    • Visit the Gaslamp Quarter in the afternoon or early evening.
    • Use the hotel food and beverage credit if it is valid on arrival day.
  2. Thu · Dec 17Pass day 1

    San Diego Zoo + Fleet Science Center

    9:00 AM–2:45 PM: San Diego Zoo
    3:00 PM–5:00 PM: Fleet Science Center

    Prioritize the Zoo’s highest-interest animal areas first; use the included bus tour or aerial tram when a break is needed. The nearby Fleet Science Center adds an interactive indoor activity after several hours of walking.

  3. Fri · Dec 18Pass day 2

    LEGOLAND + SEA LIFE Aquarium

    Reserve Go City LEGOLAND admission as soon as the date becomes available. Make this a full-day outing, visit SEA LIFE during the day rather than just before closing, and do not schedule another attraction.

    Choose a separate transportation plan: one-day rental car, COASTER plus rideshare, or direct rideshare.

  4. Sat · Dec 19Pass day 3

    SeaWorld

    Treat SeaWorld as a full-day attraction. Review showtimes and holiday-event schedules before arriving; prioritize scheduled shows, then fit rides and animal exhibits between them.

    Optional evening: Visit Belmont Park only if it is open and everyone still has energy. Verify December ride operations and the Go City benefit.

  5. Sun · Dec 20Pass day 4

    USS Midway + Balboa Park art museums

    10:00 AM–12:30 PM: USS Midway Museum
    12:30 PM–1:15 PM: Quick lunch and rideshare to Balboa Park
    1:30 PM–3:15 PM: San Diego Museum of Art
    3:20 PM–4:45 PM: Mingei International Museum

    Walk Balboa Park’s gardens and historic architecture as time permits. If time is tight, prioritize the San Diego Museum of Art rather than adding another museum.

    Possible substitutions
    • Timken Museum of Art: small and normally free; add only if time permits.
    • Fleet Science Center: move here if missed Thursday.
    • Natural History Museum: family alternative to a second art museum.
    • Air & Space Museum: an alternative for an aviation or space fan.
  6. Mon · Dec 21Departure · No pass

    Easy departure

    Have breakfast and take a short marina or Children’s Park walk. Check out and leave for the airport at approximately 11:15 AM for the 2:00 PM flight.

    Adjust for baggage, airline guidance, traffic, and holiday-period airport conditions.

Plan the journeys

Getting around

The Marriott Marquis supports a mostly car-free stay. Seaport Village, Children’s Park, the waterfront, Gaslamp Quarter, and USS Midway are walkable; nearby trolley stations serve the Convention Center and Gaslamp Quarter. Balboa Park and the Zoo are easier by bus or short rideshare.

The airport is a short rideshare from the hotel, and SeaWorld is generally simplest by rideshare. LEGOLAND and Safari Park are the main attractions where a car or more involved plan may be worthwhile. Several specific rideshares will likely cost less than renting a car for the entire stay and paying $55 daily hotel parking.

LEGOLAND options

One-day rental car

Check downtown and airport agencies, plus Zipcar, Turo, and Getaround. Airport locations may have later return hours.

Compare base rate/taxes, insurance, mileage, fuel, LEGOLAND parking, booster needs, pickup/return transport, and late-return or overnight charges.

COASTER + rideshare

Walk or rideshare to Santa Fe Depot, take COASTER north toward Carlsbad, then rideshare to LEGOLAND.

Verify the December timetable and a return train after park closing. This may be economical but is less flexible than driving.

Direct rideshare

The simplest choice, but possibly expensive—especially leaving at park closing. Check Uber and Lyft and account for surge pricing.

Travel safely

Child passenger safety

A 9-year-old may legally use a regular seat belt in California based on the current age rule, but use a booster unless the seat belt fits correctly. Do not expect Uber or Lyft to supply a booster; bring a lightweight travel booster if needed, and have the child ride in the back seat.

The five-step fit test

  1. Back rests against the vehicle seat.
  2. Knees bend naturally at the seat edge.
  3. Lap belt lies low across the hips, not the stomach.
  4. Shoulder belt crosses the chest and shoulder, not the neck.
  5. Child can maintain the position for the entire trip.
